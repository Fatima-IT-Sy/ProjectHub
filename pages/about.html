About ProjectHub

Built by a team, for teams.

ProjectHub is a course project for IT320 Practical Software Engineering at King Saud University. We are four Information Technology students who wanted to make it easier for students to find the right teammates for hackathons, competitions, and practical projects.

Why we built ProjectHub

We asked university students how they find teammates. Here is what they told us.

93.9%

said the biggest difficulty is finding people with the required skills.

87.9%

find their teammates through friends or classmates only.

81.8%

rated a Team Matching feature as very useful.

Source: ProjectHub student questionnaire, 2026.
